    How it works

    1. 1
      Make the list. Mains, sides, desserts, drinks. Say how many of each you need.
    2. 2
      Drop the link in the group chat. The preview shows what's still needed: "9 of 14 covered · still needed: 2 sides".
    3. 3
      Everyone taps a dish. Just a name, no sign-up. Duplicates get flagged ("Ana is already bringing mashed potatoes").
    4. 4
      See dietary needs at a glance. Who's vegetarian, who has a nut allergy, and which dishes fit.

    Questions

    Do my guests need an account or the app?

    No. They open the link, type their first name and tap what they'll bring. It works in any phone browser, including the one inside WhatsApp, iMessage and Messenger.

    Is it really free? Are there ads?

    Free, and no ads. We don't ask for emails or phone numbers.

    Can two people claim the same thing?

    No. Once a dish is taken it's gone, and if someone adds an extra that looks like a dish already on the list, they see a heads-up first.

    How do I manage the list from another phone?

    Everyone, host included, gets a personal link on the list page. Open it on another device and you're you again.

    What happens to the list afterwards?

    The host can delete it any time. Lists are deleted automatically 180 days after they were made. Privacy.
