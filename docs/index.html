Who's bringing what? Sorted in one link.

A free potluck sign-up list for Friendsgiving, Christmas dinners, BBQs and parties. Make the list, drop the link in WhatsApp or iMessage, and everyone taps the dish they'll bring. Duplicates get flagged, and dietary needs (vegetarian, gluten-free, nut allergy) show at a glance. No accounts, no app, no ads.
