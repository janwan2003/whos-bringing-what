Who's Bringing What

Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we store

For each list: the details the host types (event name, optional date, time, place and note), the categories and items, and for each person on the list the name they typed, any dietary needs they chose to share, and what they are bringing.

Each person gets a random key, kept in their browser's local storage, that lets them change their own entries. We store only a one-way hash of it.

To stop abuse we keep a counter per connection for up to an hour, keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address. We do not store IP addresses themselves.

What we don't do

No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no ads, no tracking cookies, no selling or sharing of data.

Who can see a list

Anyone with the link can see the list, including names and dietary needs entered on it. Only share it with your guests, and don't enter anything you wouldn't put in the group chat.

How long

The host can delete a list at any time, and anyone can remove themselves. Lists are deleted automatically 180 days after they were created.

Where

The site and its data run on Cloudflare (Pages and D1).

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue.

About

Who's Bringing What is a small free tool for planning potlucks and shared dinners.
